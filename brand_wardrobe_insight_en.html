Methodology

How to read this report

  • Of the garments photographed and analyzed at LaundryGo's facilities, those with a brand recognized by AI were linked to laundry orders and aggregated per customer. The period is June 1 to November 10, 2026.
  • A brand is recognized when its label or logo is legible in the photo; Korean and English spellings of the same brand were merged. Figures are best read as ranks and ratios rather than absolute values.
  • "All customers" means every customer with at least one brand recognized by AI during the period. Location is based on the customer's registered primary address.
  • "Co-ownership" compares customers who send a similar volume of clothes, removing the effect that heavy users appear to own more brands. Values can be very high between sub-brands of the same company.
  • "Owns" means the customer sent the brand to LaundryGo for washing at least once during the period. Where or when it was purchased is unknown.
  • Retail and resale prices are AI estimates from garment photos.
  • All figures are statistical aggregates and contain no personally identifiable information. Region–brand combinations with fewer than 30 customers are not shown.
