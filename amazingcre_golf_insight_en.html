Key findings

01 · Customers

The most active laundry customers

Customers who send golf apparel send 3.4× more garments, 2.4× more often, than customers of other brands. AMAZINGCRE customers send 1.7× more than even that golf average. Golf customers cluster in the Gangnam 3 districts (about 1 in 5 customers in Banpo-dong and Dogok-dong), while AMAZINGCRE customers skew toward Gyeonggi (36%).

02 · Competition

Overlap with MARK & LONA and G/FORE

Even against golf customers who send a similar volume of clothes, AMAZINGCRE customers are 4.8× as likely to own MARK & LONA and 2.3× as likely to own G/FORE. 1 in 9 MARK & LONA customers is already an AMAZINGCRE customer.

03 · Product

Pants are a strength, knits a gap

Pants' share of AMAZINGCRE garments is 1.5× the golf apparel average. Knits are 2.5%, about one-third of the average (8.9%). Titleist accounts for 39% of these customers' golf outerwear.

① Region

Golf customers lean toward the Gangnam 3 districts, AMAZINGCRE customers toward Gyeonggi

26% of golf customers live in Seoul's Gangnam, Seocho and Songpa districts, 1.8× the share for non-golf customers (14%). AMAZINGCRE customers match golf customers overall in the Gangnam 3 districts, but their Gyeonggi share is 36%, 1.4× that of golf customers (25%). Measured as the share of golf customers in each region who own AMAZINGCRE, Gyeonggi is also highest at 3.3%, and Seoul outside the Gangnam 3 districts is lowest at 1.5%.

Outside Seoul, new-town residential areas such as Bundang, Songdo (Yeonsu-gu), Suji, Ilsan and Hanam rank high, overlapping with the Gyeonggi areas where AMAZINGCRE customers are relatively concentrated. Pants are a strength; knits are a gap

Data criteria

How to read this report

  • Garments photographed and analyzed at LaundryGo's plant whose brand was recognized by AI were linked to laundry orders and aggregated by customer. The period is June 1 to November 9, 2026.
  • A brand is recognized when its label or logo is legible in the photo. Read the figures as ranks and multiples rather than absolute values.
  • Brand comparisons are made between golf customers who send a similar volume of clothes.
  • Regions are based on customers' registered primary address.
  • "Owning" a brand means sending it to LaundryGo for washing at least once during the period.
  • Retail and resale prices are AI estimates from garment photos.
  • All figures are statistical aggregates and contain no personally identifiable information.
